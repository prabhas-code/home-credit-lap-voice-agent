# RETELL AI TEST CALL SCRIPTS & CHEATSHEET
## Golden Call Scripts to Record for Assignment Submission

Use these scripts when making live microphone test calls in the Retell AI web dialer.

---

## 🌟 CALL 1: PERFECT ELIGIBLE LEAD (GOLDEN RECORDING)
*Use this for your primary assignment submission recording.*

- **Agent**: "Hello, am I speaking with Mr. Rajesh Sharma?"
- **You (Customer)**: "Yes, Rajesh speaking. How can I help you?"
- **Agent**: "Hi Mr. Sharma, this is Priya calling from Home Credit. We are reaching out to reward your loyalty with a special pre-approved Loan Against Property offer of up to ₹75 Lakhs. May I ask a few quick questions to check your eligibility?"
- **You**: "Sure, I have a few minutes. Go ahead."
- **Agent**: "To start, could you tell me if your property is residential, commercial, or industrial?"
- **You**: "It is a residential 3BHK apartment in Noida."
- **Agent**: "Got it. And are you the sole owner, or is it jointly owned?"
- **You**: "I am the sole owner."
- **Agent**: "Do you have the original property documents ready and available for verification?"
- **You**: "Yes, I have all the original property papers and registry with me."
- **Agent**: "How much loan amount are you looking to borrow?"
- **You**: "I am looking for around 50 lakhs."
- **Agent**: "Are you currently salaried or self-employed, and do you receive your income through a bank account or in cash?"
- **You**: "I am salaried working in an IT firm, and my salary comes directly into my bank account."
- **Agent**: "What would be the approximate current market value of your property?"
- **You**: "It should be around 1.2 crore."
- **Agent**: "Over how many years would you like to repay the loan?"
- **You**: "Around 10 years."
- **Agent**: "Thank you so much for providing all the details, Mr. Rajesh Sharma. You have successfully completed the preliminary eligibility qualification for our Loan Against Property offer! A senior loan expert from Home Credit will call you back shortly to discuss the exact interest rates and finalize your application. Thank you for your time, and have a wonderful day!"
